# Governance

Awesome Global FDE is maintained as public community infrastructure for the
Forward Deployed Engineer ecosystem.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who proposes, verifies, improves, translates, or maintains a resource.

### Reviewers

Trusted contributors who help verify links, evidence, descriptions, conflicts
of interest, and category fit.

### Maintainers

Members appointed by the Global FDE organization who can merge changes, manage
repository settings, enforce community standards, and publish releases.

## Decision principles

Maintainers make editorial decisions using these priorities, in order:

1. safety, privacy, authorization, and legal constraints;
2. direct relevance to real FDE work;
3. strength and transparency of evidence;
4. practical value to the community;
5. durability and maintainability of the resource;
6. breadth across regions, industries, organizations, and perspectives.

Inclusion does not mean endorsement. Removal does not imply that a resource is
false or without value; it may simply be out of scope, insufficiently durable,
or no longer useful to the list.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers and reviewers must disclose relevant employment, investment,
referral, sponsorship, or commercial interests. A person with a material
conflict should not be the sole approver of that submission.

Paid placement is not accepted. Sponsorship, if introduced in the future, must
be visibly separated from editorial selection and must not influence inclusion
or ranking.

## Changes to governance

Material changes to the mission, curation criteria, license, or governance
model should be proposed in a public issue and remain open for community
comment before merging, except when an urgent safety or legal response is
required.

## Enforcement and appeals

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates the contribution guide or
code of conduct. Contributors may request reconsideration through a public
issue, unless doing so would expose confidential, personal, or security-related
information.
