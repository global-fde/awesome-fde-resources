# Global FDE: Mission, Vision, and Values

## Who we are / 我们是谁

Global FDE is the trusted connection layer for the global Forward Deployed
Engineer ecosystem.

Global FDE 是全球 Forward Deployed Engineer 生态的可信连接器。我们连接企业需求方、FDE、AI 产品与工程团队、交付伙伴、技术厂商和行业专家，让知识、能力、人才与商业机会在可信关系中流动。

Awesome Global FDE is the ecosystem's open knowledge base. It turns field
experience into shared, reviewable, and reusable knowledge.

## Vision / 愿景

> A world where every meaningful AI deployment problem can find trusted people
> and capabilities—and every FDE who creates real value can be seen, connected,
> and given greater opportunity.

> 让全球每一个值得解决的 AI 落地问题，都能找到可信的人与能力；让每一位真正创造价值的 FDE，都能被看见、被连接，获得更大的机会。

## Mission / 使命

> Connect the global FDE ecosystem, turn field experience into shared
> knowledge, verify real needs and capabilities, and enable trusted
> collaboration that moves frontier AI into the real world.

> 连接全球 FDE 生态，沉淀一线知识，验证真实需求与能力，促成跨组织、跨行业、跨地区的可信协作，让前沿 AI 转化为真实世界的结果。

For this repository, that means:

- preserving high-signal knowledge about real deployment work;
- making field methods discoverable across companies, industries, and regions;
- separating evidence from opinion and production results from demos;
- helping practitioners reuse what worked—and learn from what did not;
- recognizing contributors who improve the discipline.

## Values / 价值观

### 1. Real problems first / 真实问题优先

Start with users, workflows, production environments, and measurable outcomes.
Do not use concepts as a substitute for demand or demos as a substitute for
results.

### 2. Evidence over volume / 结果胜过声量

Prefer original sources, concrete constraints, system details, adoption data,
and honest postmortems. Popularity alone is not evidence of usefulness.

### 3. Field knowledge should compound / 一线知识应当复利

A deployment should leave behind clearer methods, reusable components,
evaluation assets, and better product decisions. Knowledge becomes valuable
when another practitioner can apply it.

### 4. Trust before transaction / 信任先于交易

Commercial collaboration is welcome, but undisclosed promotion is not.
Contributors must disclose relevant employment, investment, referral, or
financial interests.

### 5. Protect the field / 保护现场信息

Customer data, code, contracts, personal information, and non-public business
details must not be shared without authorization. Cases must be appropriately
anonymized.

### 6. Global connection, local understanding / 全球连接，本地理解

Respect differences in regulation, language, industry, infrastructure, and
organizational reality. Bring global practice into local context and make
local field knowledge legible globally.

### 7. Candid and generous / 坦诚且慷慨

State uncertainty, include failures, and distinguish facts, source claims, and
personal judgment. Help another practitioner make a better decision.

## Our promise / 我们的承诺

> Bring a real deployment problem. Leave with trusted connections and reusable
> field knowledge.

> 带着真实问题来，带着可信连接和可复用经验离开。
