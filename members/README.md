# Global FDE Member Network

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

This directory helps enterprises and practitioners find people or teams with verifiable FDE capabilities.

## What a member profile may include

- FDE capabilities and delivery scope;
- industries, regions, and working languages;
- public projects and anonymized delivery evidence;
- books, guides, tools, courses, or other reusable resources;
- availability and a public contact channel;
- employment, investment, referral, or commercial disclosures.

## Member types

- **Practitioner** — an individual who performs FDE or related production AI delivery;
- **Delivery team** — a company or team that can scope, implement, deploy, and support production systems;
- **Domain expert** — an industry specialist who can support discovery, workflow design, validation, or adoption;
- **Technology partner** — a provider of infrastructure or tools with demonstrated delivery support.

## Review rules

Profiles are evidence-based, not advertisements. Claims must link to public work where possible. Confidential customer information, unverifiable rankings, pricing claims, and generic promotional copy are not accepted. Inclusion does not imply endorsement by Global FDE.
