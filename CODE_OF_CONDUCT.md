# Code of Conduct

Global FDE exists for serious, generous exchange and trusted collaboration
across companies, industries, regions, and professional backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, systems, evidence, and trade-offs—not personal worth.
- Give specific, constructive feedback with enough context to be useful.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and consent.
- Disclose commercial interests when recommending resources or organizations.
- Help make the community accessible across languages and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- publishing private information without permission;
- fabricated credentials, projects, outcomes, or customer relationships;
- undisclosed paid promotion, affiliate links, or coordinated manipulation;
- spam, repetitive self-promotion, or bulk solicitation;
- sharing confidential data, code, contracts, or security-sensitive details;
- retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces represented as part of Global FDE. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, limit participation, or block accounts when necessary.

For sensitive reports, contact a Global FDE organization owner privately. Do
not put personal, confidential, or security-sensitive evidence in a public
issue. A dedicated private reporting address will be added once organization
email infrastructure is available.

Maintainers must handle reports in good faith, protect confidentiality where
possible, and avoid adjudicating cases in which they have a direct conflict of
interest.
