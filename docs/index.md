# Awesome Global FDE

**The open knowledge base for Forward Deployed Engineers and production AI teams.**

Global FDE connects the people, capabilities, field knowledge, demand, and
opportunities that turn frontier AI into production systems and measurable
real-world outcomes.

## What you will find

- Forward Deployed Engineer role definitions and career paths;
- field discovery, technical scoping, and delivery methods;
- production AI and AI agent architectures;
- evaluation, observability, security, governance, and operations;
- real deployment cases and honest postmortems;
- English and Chinese resources for practitioners and enterprise teams.

## Our field judgment

An AI project is not successful because the model works in a demo. It succeeds
when the system enters a real workflow, is trusted and adopted, produces a
measurable outcome, and leaves behind reusable organizational capability.
